Our story

Good things, made fresh
on Western Road.

The Green Frog Café is a much-loved little spot on Cork's Western Road, just along from the Mardyke. We're all about real food and proper drinks — smoothies blended to order, speciality coffee sourced through a family tradition in Honduras, and a counter full of fresh bakes and treats.

Whether you're after a quick flat white on the way into town, a vegan smoothie after a walk in Fitzgerald's Park, or a relaxed bite in the sun, you'll always get a warm welcome here.

  • 100% eco-friendlyEvery cup, lid and straw we use is compostable.
  • Made vegan, easilyAll of our smoothies can be made fully plant-based.
  • Honduran coffeeA real family tradition, in every single cup.
